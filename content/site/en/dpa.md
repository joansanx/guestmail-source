This data processing agreement applies when you store the personal data of your guests and customers in GuestMail. You, the hotel or the restaurant, are the controller of that data, and GuestMail is your processor. It is part of the [terms of service](/terms) and follows Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. What GuestMail processes, and for how long

- **Purpose.** To keep your contact lists, send your emails, run your signup forms and your popup, and show you the results.
- **Duration.** For as long as you have a GuestMail account, and the {{deletion_grace_days}} days a deleted account can be restored.
- **People.** The guests, customers and subscribers in your lists.
- **Data.** Email address, name, language, country, birthday as day and month, a VIP mark, tags and the fields you create. The stays and reservations you import: dates, booking channel, room type, party size and amount. What happened with each email: sent, delivered, opened, clicked, bounced, marked as spam or unsubscribed. What a person types in your signup form or popup, the notice they were shown when they signed up, and the offer code they received.

## 2. Your instructions to GuestMail

GuestMail processes that data only on your documented instructions. Your instructions are this agreement, the terms of service and what you do in your account, through the dashboard, the API or the MCP server. If GuestMail believes an instruction breaks data protection law, it tells you.

## 3. Confidentiality at GuestMail

The people at GuestMail with access to your lists are bound to confidentiality, and that access is limited to those who run the service.

## 4. Security at GuestMail

GuestMail applies the measures described in the [privacy policy](/privacy): encrypted connections, access to the servers limited to the people who run the service, and API keys stored as a hash. It helps you with the security duties the GDPR puts on you, with the information it has.

## 5. Subprocessors of GuestMail

You authorize the providers listed on the [subprocessors](/subprocessors) page. GuestMail tells you by email before adding or replacing one, and you can object by closing your account. GuestMail asks each of them for the same data protection duties this agreement sets.

Some subprocessors are outside the European Economic Area. The [subprocessors](/subprocessors) page says where each one is, and on what the transfer relies.

## 6. Requests from your guests

GuestMail helps you answer the requests of the people in your lists: access, correction, deletion, objection and a copy of their data. You can see, change, export and delete a contact yourself from your account. If a guest writes to GuestMail directly, GuestMail points them to you.

## 7. Breaches

GuestMail tells you without undue delay when a breach affects your lists, with what it knows about what happened, so that you can meet your own duty to notify.

## 8. The end of the service

You can export your contacts at any time. When your account is deleted it can be restored for {{deletion_grace_days}} days. After that its newsletters, contacts, campaigns and images are deleted for good. Addresses that must not be emailed again can be kept as a one-way hash, so that the block survives without keeping the address.

## 9. Showing that GuestMail complies

GuestMail gives you the information you need to show that this agreement is met, and answers the reasonable questions of an audit you carry out or commission. Write to {{support_email}}.

## 10. Your part as the controller

You decide who is in your lists and why, and you confirm that you have a lawful basis to email each person, such as their consent where the law asks for it. The [acceptable use policy](/acceptable-use) says who can be emailed with GuestMail.
