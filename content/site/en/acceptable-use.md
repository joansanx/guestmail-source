GuestMail senders share sending addresses and a reputation with mailbox providers. One sender who emails people who never asked for it hurts every hotel and restaurant on the service. This policy is part of the [terms of service](/terms).

## Who you can email with GuestMail

- Guests and customers who gave their email address to your venue.
- People who signed up through your forms, your popup or your booking flow.
- Marketing emails need the consent of each person where the law asks for it, as it does in the European Union for most contacts.

You cannot email:

- Lists you bought, rented, borrowed or scraped from the web.
- Relay addresses that a booking platform gives you for a single stay, such as addresses at `guest.booking.com`. They are for messages about that booking, not for marketing.
- People who unsubscribed, complained or asked your venue to stop.

## What every GuestMail email carries

- A sender name and a subject that say honestly who writes and about what.
- The postal address of your venue.
- A working unsubscribe link. GuestMail adds it to every campaign, and you cannot hide or remove it.

## Content GuestMail does not send

- Phishing, malware, scams and anything built to deceive the reader.
- Emails that pretend to come from another person, brand or venue.
- Sexually explicit content, and content that promotes hate or violence.
- Offers of goods or services that are illegal where you or your readers are.

## How to use GuestMail's platform

- Do not try to get around plan limits, rate limits or a block, for example with several accounts.
- Do not test the security of the service without written permission.
- Do not resell access to your account or to the API as a service of your own.

## What GuestMail does when the policy is broken

An automated check reads a campaign's subject, sender name and content before it goes out. It never reads your contacts. When a campaign looks clearly harmful it waits, and a person at GuestMail decides whether it can be sent. More on this in [how GuestMail uses AI](/ai).

If bounces or spam complaints climb, GuestMail can slow or pause your sending until the cause is fixed. For serious or repeated abuse GuestMail can block the account or close it. A blocked account is not billed while the block lasts.

## Report abuse of GuestMail

If you received an email sent through GuestMail that you did not ask for, write to {{support_email}} and forward the email with its headers.
